How it works

Each prediction combines three things: the verifier’s public progress (streams and videos), how Pointercrate has placed and moved levels in the past (cross-checked with the AREDL and the Global Demonlist), and what top players say about difficulty. Verifiers’ own estimates are weighed against their track record, since some players consistently over- or underestimate. Where that matters, the level page flags it under Reliability.

What the labels mean

  • Very likely Expected to happen by that date unless something goes wrong.
  • Likely More likely than not.
  • Possible A real chance, but less than even.
  • Unlikely Would take a surprise.
  • Impossible Can’t happen by that date.
  • Unfinished The level isn’t finished yet.
